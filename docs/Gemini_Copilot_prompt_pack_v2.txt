C5: FIRST-PASS NATURAL PERIODS
------------------------------------------------------------

Write a new function:

natural_periods(
    model,
    added_mass_coeffs,
    slack_tanks=None,
    numerical_tolerance=...,
) -> dict

Required label:

"First-pass estimate, not a motion analysis"

HEAVE

Use:

T_heave = 2*pi*sqrt((m + a33) / C33)

where:

C33 = rho*g*Aw

and:

- m is total mass in kg
- a33 is heave added mass in kg
- rho is kg/m3
- g is m/s2
- Aw is equilibrium waterplane area in m2
- C33 is in N/m

ROLL AND PITCH

Use:

T_roll = 2*pi*sqrt((I44 + a44) / C44)

T_pitch = 2*pi*sqrt((I55 + a55) / C55)

where:

C44 = W*GM_roll   with GM_roll  = hydrostatics()["gm_y"]   (heel about the x axis)

C55 = W*GM_pitch  with GM_pitch = hydrostatics()["gm_x"]   (heel about the y axis)

Use hydrostatics()["waterplane_area"] for Aw. Do not recompute it.

and:

- I44 and I55 are rigid-body inertias in kg m2
- a44 and a55 are added inertias in kg m2
- W is weight in N
- GM is in m

MASS DATA LIMITATION

The current synthetic weights are a few lumped point masses (for example 260 t at z = 15 m), so the
roll and pitch inertia from them is a lower bound and will be too small for a 105 m jacket.
Report this in the warnings. Do not scale or adjust it.

Element does not contain material density, dry mass or mass per unit length.

Therefore:

- Calculate rigid-body inertia from WeightItem objects only.
